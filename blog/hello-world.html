Hello, world — why I'm starting to write

Four years into product management, I've noticed that the lessons I remember are the ones I had to explain to someone else. So I'm going to start explaining them here.

Product Management 26 July 2026 …

Contents

This is the first post on this site, so it is mostly a statement of intent — what I plan to write here, and why I think it is worth the effort.

Why write at all?

Most of what I have learned as a Product Manager arrived the same way: a decision that felt obvious at the time, a result that did not match, and a conversation afterwards where I had to say out loud what I had actually assumed. Writing is that conversation, minus the need to find someone patient enough to have it.

There is a selfish reason too. Product work leaves surprisingly little behind. A roadmap gets superseded, a specification gets closed, a beta programme ends and the Confluence page goes stale. The thinking is the durable part — and it is the part nobody documents.

What I'll write about

Roughly in this order:

  • Discovery — how you decide which problem is worth solving, and how to tell a strong signal from a loud one.
  • Roadmaps and prioritisation — the trade-offs that never make it onto the final slide.
  • Launches and beta programmes — what I got right and, more usefully, what I did not.
  • Working across functions — how product, sales and engineering each read the same roadmap differently.
  • The occasional detour into data science, which is where I started.

I would rather write about things I have actually done than about product management in the abstract. So expect specifics: real trade-offs from workforce management, reality capture and process mining, with the confidential parts left out.

How often

Honestly: whenever I have something worth saying. I would rather publish six posts a year that I still stand behind than one a week that I would not. If you would like to know when something new appears, the RSS feed is the reliable way — or find me on LinkedIn.

Disagreement welcome. If you think something here is wrong, that is the most useful thing you can send me. My email address is on the homepage.
